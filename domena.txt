CALCULATIONS
    - DailyChange
      - ((Price(D) - Price(D-1)) / Price(D-1)) * 100%
    - MarketCapitalization
      - Price(D) * SharesOutstanding(D)
    - PriceChange12M
      - ((Price(D) - Price(Y-1))/Price(Y-1))*100%
    - PriceChange6M
      - ((Price(D) - Price(M-6))/Price(M-6))*100%
    - PriceChange3M
      - ((Price(D) - Price(M-3))/Price(M-3))*100%
    - RelativeMomentum12M
      - ((Price(D) - Price(Y-1))/Price(Y-1))*100% - ((PriceWig(D) - PriceWig(Y-1))/PriceWig(Y-1))*100%
    - RelativeMomentum6M
      - ((Price(D) - Price(M-6))/Price(M-6))*100% - ((PriceWig(D) - PriceWig(M-6))/PriceWig(M-6))*100%
    - RelativeMomentum3M
      - ((Price(D) - Price(M-3))/Price(M-3))*100% - ((PriceWig(D) - PriceWig(M-3))/PriceWig(M-3))*100%
    - AverageOperatingProfitPerShare3Y
      - ((EBIT(D)/SharesInYear(D)) + (EBIT(Y-1)/SharesInYear(Y-1)) + (EBIT(Y-2)/SharesInYear(Y-2)))/3
    - OperatingMargin
      - (EBIT(D)/SalesRevenue(D)) * 100%
    - EV_EBITDA
      - EV(D)/(EBIT(D) + Amortization(D))
    - PriceToOperatingProfit
      - MarketCapitalization(D)/EBIT(D)
    - AverageTurnover20
      - (SessionTurnover(D) + SessionTurnover(D-1) + ... + SessionTurnover(D-19))/20
    - ROE
      - (NetIncome(D)/Equity(D)) * 100%
    - ROA
      - (NetIncome(D)/TotalAssets(D)) * 100%
    - PriceToBook
      - MarketCapitalization(D)/Equity(D)
    - TotalDebt
      - (TotalLiabilities(D)/TotalAssets(D))
